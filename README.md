## 📖 The story so far

**Chapter 1 · It started with packets 📡**<br/>
I trained as a network and telecom engineer, so my first obsession was how data travels. I taught an SDN controller to [spot DDoS floods and fight back with reinforcement learning](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/RL-based-DDos-Mitigation-in-SDN-based-broadband-networks), and later helped plan fibre and 5G networks at Omantel.

**Chapter 2 · Then I wanted to meet the attackers 🍯**<br/>
So I built them a trap: a [Kubernetes honeynet](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/Honeypot-CK8s) that lures them in and streams every move into live dashboards. As a DevSecOps engineer at Speedykom I locked down and automated an [open-source pandemic analytics platform](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/Regional-Pandemic-Analytics) for East Africa with Kubernetes, Vault, Keycloak and GitHub Actions.

**Chapter 3 · Then the data started talking 🎧**<br/>
At Bridges For Fairness I listened to materials crack, training CNNs and RNNs on acoustic-emission signals to predict failure before it happens. For fun, I [scraped four car marketplaces](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/ML-Car-Price-Prediction) to find out what a used car is really worth.

**Chapter 4 · Then I built a startup's brain from zero 🧠**<br/>
As founding AI/ML engineer at Clarity Chain, I took one idea all the way: [forecasting battery-metal prices](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/claritychains-model), hedging the risk, and an LLM agent that reads supplier offers and tells you who to pick. Concept, UX, models, backend, deployment: [**ClarityChains**](https://github.com/OnsKoukiPro/ClarityChains) is [live](https://clarity-chains.vercel.app).

**Chapter 5 · Now: powering the energy transition 🔋**<br/>
Writing my Master's thesis at ACCURE Battery Intelligence, building platform services for battery-storage customers on AWS EKS with Helm, Argo CD and Pulumi.

**Chapter 6 · ✍️ still being written.** Got an idea that needs someone who can take it end to end? [Let's talk.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ons-kouki/)
